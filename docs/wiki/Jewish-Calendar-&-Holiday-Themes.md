# 🕯️ Jewish Calendar & Holiday Themes

The system includes an autonomous Jewish calendar engine integrated with the **Hebcal GPS API**.

---

## 🕒 Autonomous Shabbat Calculations
* **GPS Accuracy:** Calculates exact candle lighting and Havdalah times based on the building's latitude & longitude.
* **Parashat HaShavua:** Displays current week's Torah portion automatically.
* **Grand Hero Shabbat Slide:** Activates automatic celebratory holiday card before Shabbat with candle times.

---

## 🎨 Automated Holiday Themes
Automatically switches wallpapers and themes on:
* 🍎 Rosh Hashanah & Yom Kippur
* 🌿 Sukkot & Simchat Torah
* 🕎 Hanukkah
* 🌾 Tu BiShvat & Purim
* 🍷 Pesach
* 🇮🇱 Memorial Day & Independence Day
* 📜 Shavuot & Tu B'Av
