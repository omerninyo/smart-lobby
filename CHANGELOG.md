All notable changes to the Smart Lobby open-source template will be documented in this file.

and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [2.6.0] - 2026-10-21
### Added
- **Environmental & Marine Data for Hadera (Air Quality & Marine API):** Integrated Open-Meteo Air Quality and Marine APIs (no API key required) in both `server.js` and `screen.js`. Enriched weather model with European Air Quality Index (`aqi` + color-coded status), UV radiation index (`uvIndex` + severity level), sea wave height and water temperature for Hadera coast (Olga beach), and smart automated daily tips for tenants (`dailyTip`). Rendered as a compact 4-card environmental grid on the weather forecast slide and in the interactive weather modal.
- **Transit & Commute Slide (`transit_commute`):** Added dynamic public transit and traffic dashboard for residents leaving the building. Features real-time timetable estimations for Israel Railways Hadera West station (South/Tel Aviv and North/Haifa with Shabbat awareness), key municipal and intercity bus routes (lines 7, 15, 22, 704), and live highway commute drive times (Road 2 coastal highway, Road 4, Sharon, and Caesarea Industrial Park) with dynamic peak-hour congestion color badges. Controlled via admin toggle `#setting-show-transit-slide`.
- **Live Hebrew Calendar Header & Badges:** Integrated accurate Hebrew date rendering under the digital clock (`#clock-date-greg`) using `Intl.DateTimeFormat` with traditional Hebrew letter numerics (e.g. `יום רביעי, 21 באוקטובר • י' בחשוון תשפ"ז`). Added special contextual calendar badges in the header center and Shabbat modal for Sefirat HaOmer, Hanukkah candles, and Rosh Chodesh.
- **Permanent Building Cleaning Schedule & Item-Clearing Notice (`notice-cleaning-schedule`):** Added a configurable permanent cleaning schedule notice in rotation (`data/notices.json`, `admin.html`, `js/admin.js`, `js/screen.js`). Includes an interactive Cleaning Days panel (`#cleaning-schedule-panel`) in Admin Tab 1 allowing 1-click selection of weekly cleaning days (default: Sunday & Thursday `[0, 4]`), automatically calculating the previous-evening preparation schedule (`במוצאי שבת וביום רביעי בערב`) instructing residents to clear all items from hallways and the lobby (doormats/rugs, strollers, bicycles, shoes, and bags). Highlights automatically on the lobby screen on preparation evenings and cleaning days.
- **Daily Interest & Trivia Slide (`daily_interest`):** Implemented a 40-set rich infotainment & science pool (`DAILY_INTEREST_SETS`) with continuous per-slide rotation (`refreshDailyInterestSlide` + coprime index mixing across 3 cards = 64,000 combinations), refreshing automatically on every slideshow visit and every 2 minutes, plus touch-to-refresh on the slide. Each slide features: "Did You Know? / Science & History" (space, science, history, nature), an engaging trivia question with highlighted answer box, and fascinating "Science in Everyday Life" facts (replacing boring building rules). Controlled via admin toggle `#setting-show-interest-slide`.
- **Automatic Pikud HaOref Emergency Alerts:** Added automated silent background poller (`startOrefAlertPoller`, checking every 15 seconds) verifying active Home Front Command alerts for Hadera via `/api/oref` (with allorigins CORS proxy fallback on static hosting). Automatically triggers high-contrast emergency shelter instructions, immediately mutes background radio stream (`stopRadioImmediate()`), and automatically schedules a 10-minute return to routine operation following the end of an alert. Configurable in admin emergency panel via `#emergency-auto-oref`.
- **Modular News & Updates Ticker Builder (`#modular-news-matrix`):** Upgraded the news ticker into a fully modular multi-channel system in Tab 2 (`admin.html`, `js/admin.js`, `js/screen.js`). Administrators can now choose from quick presets (`smart_mix`, `news_all`, `science_tech`, `economy`, `building_only`, or single sources) OR toggle individual checkboxes (`newsChannels`) across 14 live channels: **News (`ynet`, `ynet_mivzak`, `mako`, `walla`, `maariv`)**, **Economy (`globes`, `ynet_econ`)**, **Science & Tech (`hayadan`, `geektime`, `ynet_tech`)**, and **Local Building & Community Updates (`building_notices`, `local_weather`, `shabbat_calendar`, `daily_trivia`)**, plus a toggleable anti-clickbait filter (`filterClickbait`) and a custom RSS URL field (`customRssUrl`). Powered by direct CORS-open WordPress JSON APIs + real-time `r.jina.ai` XML extraction (`extractXmlRssTitles`) + `allorigins` fallback, progressive rendering, `localStorage` live cache, source badges (`.ticker-source-tag`), and dynamic marquee speed scaling.
- **Enriched Notice Image Pools & Zero-Overhead Dynamic Variety (`resolveNoticeImageUrl`):** Expanded `notice_topics` in `data/wallpapers.json` from 6 to 14 vertical (3:4) images, adding 3 new dog breeds (`pets_french_bulldog_lobby.jpg`, `pets_poodle_garden.jpg`, `pets_border_collie_park.jpg`), 3 new building/lobby scenes (`modern_israeli_building_exterior.jpg`, `luxury_boutique_lobby_warm.jpg`, `evening_building_courtyard.jpg`), plus dedicated elevator (`modern_elevator_interior.jpg`) and underground parking (`clean_underground_parking.jpg`) scenes. Optimized all 15 notice images to `750px` width (`101KB–255KB` per file, reducing total folder size from 4.9MB to 2.8MB) and added `resolveNoticeImageUrl()` in `js/screen.js` to deterministically diversify default dog and lobby notices across slides and cycles with zero extra memory or network load (`decoding="async"`, hourly tick in Lite Mode).
- **1920x1080 Full-Stage Scaling & Proportional Layout for Info Panels (`weather_forecast`, `transit_commute`, `daily_interest`):** Overhauled typography, icon sizes, card padding, and flex/grid proportions in `css/style.css` and `js/screen.js` so all three stage panels fill 100% of the vertical stage height on `1920x1080` displays with zero dead space and crystal-clear distance readability:
  - **Weather & Environment (`weather_forecast`):** Stretched `.forecast-cards-grid` (`flex: 1.15`) and converted `.forecast-env-grid` from a cramped 4-column strip into a spacious 2×2 grid (`grid-template-columns: repeat(2, 1fr); flex: 0.9;`) with large icons (`clamp(3.9rem, 7vh, 4.9rem)`) and bold values (`clamp(1.42rem, 2.65vh, 1.9rem)`).
  - **Transit & Commute (`transit_commute`):** Added `.transit-trains-body`, `.transit-train-section` (`flex: 1`), and `.transit-train-footer-box` so the Israel Railways column fills the full card height; enlarged train departure times (`clamp(1.75rem, 3.25vh, 2.35rem)`), bus badges (`clamp(1.35rem, 2.45vh, 1.75rem)`), and highway commute rows while removing ellipsis truncation on bus destinations.
  - **Daily Interest & Trivia (`daily_interest`):** Added `.interest-card-body-box` (`flex: 1`) and `.interest-card-footer-pill` across History & Science cards; enlarged `.interest-card-text` from `0.95rem` to `clamp(1.42rem, 2.75vh, 1.92rem)` (`~31px` on 1080p) and `.trivia-question` to `clamp(1.6rem, 3.1vh, 2.18rem)` (`~35px` on 1080p).
- **Automatic Morning Radio Resume & Overnight Audio Unlock Preservation (`setupWatchdog`, `stopRadioImmediate`, `startSilentNightKeepAlive`):** Fixed the issue where the radio was muted every morning at `06:30` waiting for a screen touch (`NotAllowedError`):
  - Replaced the destructive `window.location.reload()` calls at `23:05` and `04:00 AM` in `setupWatchdog()` with `performNightSoftMaintenanceSweep()`, which refreshes all data, slides, and stylesheets in-memory without destroying the browser `Document` or wiping out Sticky User Activation (`navigator.userActivation.hasBeenActive`).
  - Added an in-memory 0-volume digital-zero PCM WAV keep-alive loop (`getSilentAudioUri()` + `startSilentNightKeepAlive()`) + WebAudio `AudioContext` keep-alive (`ensureAudioContextUnlocked()`) during night hours (`23:00`–`06:30`) so Android WebView / Chromium never releases `AudioFocus` or revokes playback authorization overnight while consuming zero network bandwidth and producing zero speaker output.
  - Added a muted-to-unmuted Media Engagement Index (MEI) bridge in `startRadioStream()` and separated transient network/stream errors from `NotAllowedError` so temporary stream hiccups automatically retry instead of displaying the tap prompt.

  - Capped `#ticker-content` DOM items in `liteMode` (`rk3368`) to rotating batches of 10 items (`~6,000px` wide) to stay below the PowerVR G6110 `8192px` hardware GPU texture width limit.
